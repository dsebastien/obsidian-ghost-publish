# 2026-11-10

## Optimistic triage and queue actions

Clicking Ignore / Publish / Publish + email (triage) or Remove from queue (queue) waited for `processFrontMatter` to finish before the card started to go away. On a cloud-synced vault that write is slow, so the panel felt stuck.

### What was done

- `services/pending-mutations.ts` (+ spec): tracks notes hidden by an in-flight optimistic action. An entry is dropped on the first metadataCache `changed` event after the write resolves, after a safety timeout, or at once on failure. Follows renames.
- `plugin.ts`: owns the store, wires `metadataCache.on('changed')` and `vault.on('rename')`, and exposes `applyOptimistically(path, write)`.
- Triage and queue pages: hide the card, update the count and show the Notice on click; then write. Lists filter out pending paths so a refresh, range change, search or tab switch cannot resurrect the card. A failed write re-renders the panel and shows a Notice.
- `card-animations.ts`: the removing class is added in the same frame as the click (also blocks double clicks); fade 60 ms, collapse 150 ms.
- `incremental-list.ts`: triage and queue cards render in batches of 40; the next batch loads when a sentinel nears the viewport (IntersectionObserver, 600 px margin). The "All" range matched 5,321 notes in my vault, and the full DOM made every reflow cost ~100 ms.
- BR-UI-3 rewritten, BR-UI-4 amended, BR-UI-9 added.

### Measured (live vault, 5,321 candidates, stubbed write)

- Click to card hidden: 103 ms → 2.4 ms.
- Render of the "All" range: 150 ms (most of it is `findCandidates`).
- A refresh during the write does not bring the card back.

### Decisions

- Optimistic hide over speeding up the write: the write's speed depends on the file system, which the plugin does not control.
- Pending entries clear only when the cached frontmatter shows the write, not on the write promise (`processFrontMatter` resolves before the cache updates, BR-UI-6) and not on the first 'changed' event.

## Fix: ignored cards came back while the vault was indexing

First version dropped a pending entry on the first 'changed' event after the write, or after 3 s. While Obsidian indexed the vault, the cache lagged behind the write, so the entry expired and a tab switch showed the old list again.

- `PendingMutations.begin` takes an `isApplied(path)` check; entries drop only when it returns true (on settle or on a 'changed' event). 10 min leak guard.
- `triage-actions.ts`: `isTriageActionApplied` and `isRemovedFromQueue` (+ spec).
- `plugin.applyOptimistically` takes the `TFile` (its path follows renames during the write).
- Live check: stubbed write, two stale 'changed' events, tab switch: the card stays hidden.
