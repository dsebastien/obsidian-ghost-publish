# 2026-10-23

## Sync failures now say why

A sync against a Ghost(Pro) site over its member cap failed every new note with a `403 HostLimitError`, but the only feedback was "❌ News: errors — 8 failed". The per-note reasons were computed and then dropped (logging is a no-op in release builds).

### What was done

- `api/ghost-api-client.ts`: `GhostApiError` detail appends Ghost's `errors[0].context` (the actionable part, e.g. the plan limit and upgrade hint).
- `utils/failure-reasons.fn.ts` (+ spec): groups failed results by reason, most frequent first, capped at 3.
- `plugin.ts`: when a run has failures, the summary Notice lists the grouped reasons and stays until dismissed (`FAILURE_NOTICE_TIMEOUT_MS = 0`).

### Decisions

- Surfaced in the Notice, not in logs: logs are invisible to users and the community review discourages console logging.
- Kept BR-SYNC-5: an account-level error still runs through every note rather than aborting the batch.
